## What Should Buyers Check Before Purchasing?

Before finalizing a plot, buyers should consider:

* Location and road connectivity
* Plot size and dimensions
* Residential land-use status
* Ownership and title documents
* Nearby schools, hospitals and markets
* Water, electricity and other basic facilities
* Total purchase and registration costs

## How to Choose the Right Location?

A good residential location should provide convenient access to everyday facilities. Buyers can compare different localities based on connectivity, surrounding development, accessibility and their future construction plans.

## Why Verify Documents?

Property documents should be checked carefully before making a payment or signing an agreement. Buyers should verify ownership, title, land records, applicable approvals and plot boundaries. Professional legal verification can be useful before completing the purchase.
